# Portfolio (vinayrp.in)

The personal portfolio of Vinay Reddy Patil: a student full-stack developer focused on AI applications. The site presents his projects, technical writing, and achievements to recruiters, hackathon judges, and peers. It is a fork-and-strip conversion of ncdai/chanhdai.com (MIT), rebranded and reduced to portfolio-only scope.

## Language

### Site sections

**Project**:
A software artifact Vinay built and is showing off, with live demo and source links. The primary credibility signal on the site; leads the home page.
_Avoid_: work, experience (reserved for employment, which Vinay does not have yet)

**Achievement**:
External recognition of Vinay's work — hackathon placements, publication milestones. Shown as a visible section on the home page.
_Avoid_: testimonial (dropped concept)

**Writing**:
Vinay's published technical articles. At launch these are External Posts; the site links out rather than hosting them.

**External Post**:
An article published on Hashnode (`vinayrp-dev.hashnode.dev`); the site links to it but does not host it.

**Self-hosted Post**:
An MDX article whose canonical home is vinayrp.in. Phase 2: the strongest External Posts migrate into Self-hosted Posts.

### Excluded concepts

These exist in the upstream repo but are out of scope for this project:

- **Registry / Blocks / Component docs**: upstream's shadcn distribution system — deleted
- **Testimonials**: peer quotes from college projects read weak — dropped
- **Sponsors page**: personal monetization — dropped
- **Timeline page**: career-history page for employed developers — dropped
- **Work experience / Jobs**: Vinay is a pre-employment student — not represented
